A short presentation :

This project was made as part of the Emotional Game Jam 2026. Hasn’t been updated.

The two themes for this edition were: “SABOTAGE” and “CRINGE”
We decided to go with a 3D sabotage game where you play an alien bartender who has to destroy the bar without getting caught by the boss.
Our intention was to make a fun and entertaining game.

It was made on Unity.
The development of the game is done in C#.
